    Decide how to incorporate

    Provincial stays cheaper and simpler while you're mostly selling in Alberta; federal protects the name across the whole country but adds an extra annual filing.

    $200 federal vs. $275 Alberta
    Corporations Canada — Federal Incorporation →
  2. 8

    Register in every new province

    Extra-provincial registration is required in each additional province you actually do business in — even for a federally incorporated company, and even in your own home province.

    one filing per province, every time
    Founder's note This is the one step in the whole journey that still felt like doing the same paperwork from scratch in every province, with no federal pass-through — even after the 2026 trade and labour mobility reforms.
    If you're in the trades Your certification itself moves faster than your paperwork: Red Seal already grants national recognition without retesting, and as of August 27, 2026 every province agreed to recognize skilled trades certifications from other jurisdictions within 30 days. That's real progress for the person — it just doesn't extend to registering the business itself in each new province.

    There's no single national page for this — each province runs its own extra-provincial registration process. Start from your home registry: Alberta.ca — Find a Business Registry →

  3. 9

    Track provincial sales tax

    GST applies nationwide once registered, but what sits on top of it varies: BC, Saskatchewan, and Manitoba add a separate PST; Quebec adds QST; Ontario and the Atlantic provinces use a combined HST.

    Alberta: GST only, no PST
    CRA — GST/HST Rates by Province →

    Stay compliant as you grow

    Annual returns (provincial and/or federal), a T2 corporate tax filing every year regardless of profit, and a renewed municipal licence in every city with a physical presence.
